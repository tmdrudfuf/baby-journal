Privacy policy

Baby Journal is a private family journal for parents and caregivers. Last updated: [date].

What we collect

  • Your email address and password (stored hashed) to sign you in.
  • Names you enter: your display name in the family and your baby's name and birth date.
  • What you add to the journal: notes, photos, comments, hearts, milestones and tracker logs (feeding, sleep, diapers, growth).

We do not collect your location (location data is removed from uploaded photos), contacts, advertising IDs or device identifiers.

Who can see it

Only the people you invite into your family. This is enforced by our database, not just the app. Photos are only reachable through short-lived private links.

What we never do

No advertising. We never sell your data or use it to profile your family.

AI suggestions

If AI suggestions are enabled, the text of a single note, its date and your baby's age are sent to our AI provider (Anthropic) to suggest a journal line or detect a milestone. Photos and other memories are not sent. Suggestions are only suggestions; your original words are always kept.

Service providers

We use Supabase (database and sign-in), Cloudflare R2 (photo storage) and Anthropic (optional AI suggestions) to run the service. They process data only on our behalf.

Your choices

  • Download all your data: Family → Privacy → Download my data.
  • Delete a memory, your whole family, or your account at any time in the app, or on the account deletion page.
  • Deleting your account removes your login and profile. A family where you are the only member is deleted with everything in it. Families other people belong to are never deleted: you leave, ownership passes to the next parent or caregiver if needed, and the memories you added stay with the family without your name.
