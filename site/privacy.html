Privacy policy

Baby Journal is a private family journal for parents and caregivers. Last updated: 2026-09-27.

What we collect

  • Your email address and password (stored hashed) to sign you in.
  • Names you enter: your display name in the family and your baby's name and birth date.
  • What you add to the journal: notes, photos, short videos, comments, hearts, milestones and tracker logs (feeding, sleep, diapers, growth).

We do not collect your location (location data is removed from uploaded photos; for videos, only a still frame is re-encoded, so keep location recording off in your camera if you do not want it stored), contacts, advertising IDs or device identifiers.

Who can see it

Only the people you invite into your family. This is enforced by our database, not just the app. Photos are only reachable through short-lived private links.

What we never do

No advertising. We never sell your data or use it to profile your family.

AI suggestions

If AI suggestions are enabled, the text of a single note, its date and your baby's age are sent to our AI provider (OpenAI, with requests marked not to be stored) to suggest a journal line or detect a milestone. Photos and other memories are not sent. If you ask for a Daily Story, that day's notes are sent the same way, and if you ask your journal a question, the question and a few matching notes are sent to answer it (questions are not stored). To make search work, the text of each note is also turned into a search index on our own servers; this does not involve a third party. Suggestions are only suggestions; your original words are always kept. The family owner can turn AI suggestions off in the Family tab, and then nothing is sent.

Subscriptions

If a family owner subscribes, Google Play handles the payment; we never see card details. We receive and store which plan was bought, its status and renewal date, and Google's purchase token so we can confirm renewals with Google. Ending a subscription never deletes memories.

Service providers

We use Supabase (database and sign-in), Cloudflare R2 (photo storage), OpenAI (optional AI suggestions) and Google Play (subscriptions) to run the service. They process data only on our behalf.

Your choices

  • Download all your data: Family → Privacy → Download my data.
  • Delete a memory, your whole family, or your account at any time in the app, or on the account deletion page.
  • Deleting your account removes your login and profile. A family where you are the only member is deleted with everything in it. Families other people belong to are never deleted: you leave, ownership passes to the next parent or caregiver if needed, and the memories you added stay with the family without your name.
